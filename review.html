Framekeeper

Green = keep, red = reject. Click a shot to make it the only keep in its group (ungrouped photos: click toggles keep/reject), ⌘/Ctrl/⇧ + click to toggle it on its own (keep several). "Reject all" rejects a whole group. Preview without changing the selection: hover + Space, ⌥ + click, or the button. G opens the gallery (at the hovered group). "Compare" shows all shots of a group side by side at full resolution. Uncertain groups (orange) are listed first.
